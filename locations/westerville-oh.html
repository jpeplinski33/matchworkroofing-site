Local Knowledge

Homes & Roofs in Westerville

Westerville mixes early-1900s homes around Uptown with post-war ranches and extensive 1980s–2000s construction on the northeast side. That range means no single roofing answer: older homes may need ventilation and flashing work, while newer ones are often straightforward replacements of aging builder-grade shingles. Homes near Hoover Reservoir see real wind exposure off the water.

Older Homes Near Uptown

Early-1900s construction often means plank decking, layered roofing history, and attic ventilation that predates modern practice. The scope should address what the tear-off reveals.

Wind Exposure Near Hoover

Homes east toward the reservoir take wind off open water. Starter course adhesion and edge metal details matter on these exposures.

Straightforward Replacements Done Right

Even a simple replacement deserves a complete system: leak barrier, synthetic underlayment, starter, and balanced ventilation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work on older Westerville homes near Uptown?

Yes. Older homes can have plank decking, multiple existing layers, or outdated ventilation. Your written proposal will address what the roof actually needs.

My Westerville home is 20 years old with the original roof. What should I expect?

Most builder-grade roofs of that age are at or past design life. An inspection with photos will show remaining service life and whether repair or replacement makes sense.

Can you handle gutters at the same time as the roof?

Yes. Roofing, siding, gutters, and masonry are handled by one company, so drip edge, gutter apron, and downspout sizing are detailed together.
