About Matchwork

A Roofing Company Built Around the Details

Matchwork Roofing & Exteriors LLC serves Columbus and Central Ohio with residential roofing, siding, gutters, and masonry.

The Name

Why "Matchwork"

The name means two things. First, materials matched to the architecture of the home — the shingle profile, color, and metal details that belong on your house, not just the ones on the truck. Second, work that matches the written proposal: the scope we price is the scope we install, and we photograph both.

It also nods to the insurance work we do. When storm damage raises the question of whether a repair can match the existing roof, careful documentation — sometimes including independent laboratory identification of the shingle — is how the answer gets settled on facts.

Written, Itemized Proposals

Every proposal lists materials, components, and price — including underlayment, flashing, ventilation, protection, and cleanup. No vague one-liners.

Photo Documentation

We photograph what we find and what we install. On storm work, documentation is the product; on every other job, it's how you see what you paid for.

Materials Matched to the Home

We review physical samples against your brick, stone, siding, and trim. The right shingle on the wrong house is still the wrong shingle.

The Intersections Are Ours

Roofing, siding, gutters, and masonry meet at flashing, drip edge, and counter-flashing. One company owns those details, start to finish.

Working With Us

What to Expect

An assessment of the roof and its details. Physical material samples reviewed against your exterior. A written, itemized proposal. Protection and cleanup in the scope. Photos of the finished details — including the ones you'll never see from the ground.

Before work begins, we're glad to provide proof of insurance and documentation of manufacturer credentials on request.

Four Trades, One Contractor

Roofing, siding, gutters, and masonry handled by one company — so the places where they meet are detailed together, once.
