The Matchwork Standard

The Wall Is Part of the Roof System

Most siding failures we see are not siding failures — they are flashing failures where the roof meets the wall. Because Matchwork handles roofing, siding, gutters, and masonry, those intersections are planned in one scope instead of traded between contractors.

Step & Kick-Out Flashing

Where a roof edge meets a wall, kick-out flashing directs water into the gutter instead of behind the siding. This small detail prevents some of the most expensive hidden rot we see.

Housewrap & Flashing Layers

Water that gets past siding has to be managed by the layers behind it. Housewrap laps, window flashing, and trim details decide whether a wall stays dry.

Trim, Soffit & Fascia

The edges of a siding project — corners, window trim, soffit, and fascia — are what separate a clean installation from a rough one. They also tie directly into the roof edge and gutters.

Materials

Vinyl & Fiber Cement, Chosen for the House

Vinyl siding offers low maintenance and broad color choice. Fiber cement offers sharper profiles, deeper shadow lines, and strong fire and impact resistance. We review physical samples against your roof color, brick, and trim before you commit.

  • Color-matched trim and accessories
  • Housewrap and flashing details itemized in the proposal
  • Soffit, fascia, and gutter coordination

Signs Your Siding Needs Attention

Cracked or loose panels, fading beyond cleaning, swollen or soft spots near the roof line, and staining below missing kick-out flashing are all worth a look.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can new siding be installed over my existing siding?

It depends on the condition and type of the existing material. Tear-off exposes the wall sheathing and flashing, which is usually the better basis for a long-lived installation. Your written proposal will state which approach is assumed.

What siding materials do you install?

Vinyl and fiber cement siding, along with the trim, soffit, and fascia details that complete the wall. Material choice depends on the home's architecture and your maintenance preferences.

Does siding work affect my roof?

Where siding meets the roof — step flashing, kick-out flashing, and headwall trim — the two trades overlap. Handling both under one scope means those intersections are detailed together.

Planning a Siding Project?

Get a written, itemized proposal that covers the wall, the flashing, and the trim — not just the panels.
